Philosophers have stood nearly alone in claiming that &quot;trust in AI&quot; and &quot;trustworthy AI&quot; is conceptual nonsense. In this empirical paper we find instead that it is perfectly coherent and that philosophers may have lost the plot on &quot;trust&quot;.
